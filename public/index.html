Data source
—

            
            

            
The server polls http://energymeter.local/api/readings every 2 seconds (no ESP32 firmware change needed). Energy counters are integrated server-side from active power until the meter's energy registers are decoded.

          

          

            
Database & export
—

            

            
            
            
            
Every sample (device and simulator) is stored in SQLite at —. Raw export is capped at 100,000 rows — pick a shorter range or a coarser granularity for big exports.

          

          

            
Firmware update
—

            

            
            

              
              
            

            

            
Upload stages the firmware; Install Update atomically activates the release feed. The ESP32 picks it up on its next OTA poll and confirms back with its reported version.

About

            
    
              
  • Server: plain Node.js, no dependencies. Static assets (Chart.js, Inter font) are vendored — works fully offline on the LAN.
    • 
              
  • History: 1 s resolution for 2 hours, 1 min aggregates for 7 days, kept in memory.
    • 
              
  • Device: TPM-04-100D three-phase analyzer, read by an ESP32 over Modbus RTU (USB / RS-485).
    • 
              
  • Roadmap: 1) GUI (this build) → 2) ESP32 data ingestion → 3) register-map verification → 4) persistence + alerts.
